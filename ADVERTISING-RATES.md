# Suggested advertising packages — RENOSPAC website

A starting proposal for the "Faites de la publicité ici" pitch on the site. These are
reasonable *suggested* rates for a nonprofit-network site with modest but targeted
traffic (health facility owners, partners, MSHP-adjacent audience) — adjust to your
market and running costs before quoting anyone. Prices in Guinean Franc (GNF), billed
monthly, no long-term contract required.

| Package | Price / month | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | 500 000 GNF | Logo + link in the ad-slot rotation (homepage + Actualités page) |
| **Silver** | 1 000 000 GNF | Everything in Bronze + one sponsored post on the Actualités page per month |
| **Gold** | 2 000 000 GNF | Everything in Silver + priority placement (larger banner) + one shout-out on the RENOSPAC Facebook Page |

**One-off option:** a single sponsored article/announcement on Actualités, no
recurring placement — e.g. 300 000–400 000 GNF per post.

**Notes**
- These numbers are a reasonable starting point, not researched local market rates —
  sanity-check against what similar Guinean association/NGO sites or local ad brokers
  charge before publishing them anywhere public.
- Keep it simple at launch: even just "Bronze only" is a fine way to start and see
  what partners are willing to pay before building out tiers.
- All the "advertise here" tiles already link straight to the contact form, so a lead
  can reach you regardless of whether pricing is published on-site.
